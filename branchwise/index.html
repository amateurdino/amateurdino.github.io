Branchwise™

Record family stories before they're lost.

In development for iPhone and iPad

What it does

  • Guided interview prompts for talking with relatives
  • On-device transcription, so recordings stay on your device
  • Tag everyone mentioned in a story
  • Save stories to your family tree on FamilySearch

Privacy

Recordings and transcripts are processed on your device. Nothing is shared with FamilySearch unless you choose to upload it. A full privacy policy will be published before release.
